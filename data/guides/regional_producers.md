# Top crop-producing regions of Ghana

This note lists the leading producing regions for Ghana's major crops, so the
assistant can answer "which regions produce the most X" with real figures.

Food-crop figures are the three-year average annual production (2018–2020), in
metric tonnes (MT), from the Statistics, Research and Information Directorate
(SRID), Ministry of Food and Agriculture (MoFA), published in *Agriculture in
Ghana: Facts and Figures (2020)*. Cocoa is grown and reported separately (by
COCOBOD), so its leading regions are listed qualitatively from the sources noted.

## Maize

Top five maize-producing regions in Ghana (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Ashanti — 476,678
2. Eastern — 458,411
3. Bono — 291,293
4. Central — 283,513
5. Bono East — 222,274

The leading maize regions are therefore Ashanti and Eastern, followed by Bono,
Central and Bono East. (Source: SRID, MoFA, Agriculture in Ghana Facts & Figures
2020, Appendix 17.)

## Rice

Top five rice-producing regions (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Volta — 189,376
2. Northern — 132,175
3. Oti — 132,019
4. Upper East — 120,393
5. Ashanti — 65,217

Rice production is led by the Volta and Northern regions. (Source: SRID, MoFA,
Facts & Figures 2020, Appendix 18.)

## Cassava

Top five cassava-producing regions (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Eastern — 5,733,536
2. Ashanti — 3,649,213
3. Central — 3,037,670
4. Bono — 1,886,187
5. Bono East — 1,783,857

Cassava is dominated by the Eastern region, then Ashanti and Central. (Source:
SRID, MoFA, Facts & Figures 2020, Appendix 20.)

## Yam

Top five yam-producing regions (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Bono East — 2,273,369
2. Northern — 1,678,149
3. Upper West — 905,384
4. Eastern — 884,645
5. Ashanti — 680,018

(Source: SRID, MoFA, Facts & Figures 2020, Appendix 21.)

## Plantain

Top five plantain-producing regions (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Ashanti — 1,650,810
2. Ahafo — 954,488
3. Eastern — 881,616
4. Western North — 787,557
5. Bono — 275,282

## Sorghum

Top five sorghum-producing regions (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Upper East — 106,794
2. Upper West — 85,259
3. Northern — 82,516
4. North East — 23,859
5. Savannah — 23,603

## Cowpea

1. Upper West — 92,262
2. Northern — 51,613
3. Upper East — 31,460
4. North East — 26,856
5. Savannah — 16,164

## Soybean

Top five soybean-producing regions (three-year average, 2018–2020, MT):

1. Northern — 93,215
2. North East — 27,265
3. Upper West — 27,187
4. Upper East — 23,428
5. Savannah — 14,743

## Cocoa

Cocoa is Ghana's main export crop and is grown in the forested areas of the
country. The cocoa-producing regions are the Western Region and Western North
Region (the largest producers), Ashanti, Ahafo, Central, Eastern and Volta
regions. Ghana is the world's second-largest cocoa producer after Côte d'Ivoire.
(Sources: Agriculture in Ghana; Wikipedia, Agriculture in Ghana. Regional cocoa
tonnage is reported by COCOBOD, not in the MoFA SRID food-crop tables.)
