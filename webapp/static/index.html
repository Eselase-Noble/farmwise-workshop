Know what to plant. Get answers you can trust.

FarmWise recommends a crop from your field's soil and weather, and answers farming questions from real guides. It runs entirely on local AI models, so it works offline once it is set up.

From a field reading to a confident decision

Three steps, start to finish.

Step 1

Measure your field

Enter the soil nutrients and weather you can measure: nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, temperature, humidity, pH and rainfall.

Step 2

The model weighs it up

A Random Forest trained on thousands of records compares your field with the conditions each crop prefers.

Step 3

Act, and ask

See the recommended crop and how sure the model is, then ask follow-up questions answered from trusted farming guides.

Two tools, one assistant

A classic machine-learning model for recommendations, and a retrieval assistant for questions.

Turn seven field readings into a crop recommendation, with the model's confidence, the runner-up crops, and a warning when your inputs fall outside what it learned.
